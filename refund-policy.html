Refund Policy

At Adstube, customer satisfaction is our priority. We strive to deliver high-quality results through our professional YouTube video promotion services. However, due to the nature of digital marketing and third-party ad systems, certain limitations apply.

Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds may be approved only under the following conditions:

  • The service has not been initiated within 48 hours of order confirmation.
  • A technical issue on our end prevented your campaign from starting.
  • Duplicate payments were made for the same order.

Once a campaign has begun – meaning ads have been placed or the promotion has started – refunds cannot be processed, as advertising costs are immediately incurred.

Non-Refundable Conditions

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days via the original mode of payment.
